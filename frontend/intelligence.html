What "AI-driven" means here, honestly: (1) Pattern & anomaly detection is real statistics on accumulated history — z-scores against a commodity's own baseline, year-over-year correlation for seasonality. (2) Projections are a simple trend model that must pass its own backtest before being shown — if it fails, you see the backtest error, not a fabricated forecast. (3) Publish timing is a transparent rule combining the five PRD scoring dimensions. (4) The system "learns" by logging its own calls and checking 14 days later whether they held up, nudging its own scoring weights — a real, visible feedback loop, not a black box.
